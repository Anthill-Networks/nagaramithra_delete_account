Account and Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request the deletion of your Nagara Mitra account and all associated data. Please read the information below carefully before proceeding.

What will be deleted?

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently and irreversibly removed:

  • Your personal profile information (Name, phone number, email address, profile picture).
  • Your complete trip and ride history.
  • Saved locations (Home, Work, etc.).
  • Payment information and transaction history.

How to Request Deletion

To request the deletion of your account, please send an email to our support team by clicking the link below. Please include the phone number or email address associated with your Nagara Mitra account in the body of the email to help us identify you.

Request Account Deletion

Please note: The deletion process is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your data is deleted, you will not be able to recover it. The process may take up to 30 days to complete after we have verified your request.
